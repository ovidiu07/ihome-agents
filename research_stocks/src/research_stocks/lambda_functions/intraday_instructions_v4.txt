### 1 — Refresh today’s forecast *and* quantify edge

Using `intraday_json` up to **last\_updated\_utc**:

1. Compute intraday **HIGH\_NEW**, **LOW\_NEW**, **CLOSE\_NEW** exactly as before.
2. Derive **probabilistic ranges** for the *rest* of the day strictly from `intraday_json` and, if available, `ml_nowcast`:

   * `p10`, `p50`, `p90` of the remaining‑session close.
   * Same quantiles for high & low.
   * `probability_up`  = P(CLOSE\_final > OPEN\_BASE).
   * `probability_down` = 1 − probability\_up.
3. Estimate an **expected move (%)** = √(IV² + realised\_vol\_5d²) × √(hours\_left/6.5).
   *If IV is unavailable, write `"no data"` for `expected_move_pct`.*
4. **Compute 5‑minute OHLC forecasts for the next 45 minutes (nine bars)** using only:

   * the last two 5‑minute bars in `intraday_json`, **and**
   * the `expected_move_pct` from Step 3.
     Apply linear interpolation; cap highs and lows inside the `p10`–`p90` bands from Step 2.
5. Leave the `weekly_forecast` unchanged unless the inputs contain a new value; do **not** invent.
