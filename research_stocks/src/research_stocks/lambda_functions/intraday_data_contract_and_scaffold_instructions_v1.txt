## Anchor precedence, indexing & consistency
Authoritative anchors (use as truth if present):

- LO_SESSION = intraday_json.session_low   (do not recompute)

Index k:
1) Let t[] be fintech_one_minute.candles.t. Let T* = intraday_json.fintech_one_minute.last_updated_utc.
2) If ∃i: t[i] == T* then k=i; else k = max{i | t[i] ≤ T*}; if none, k=len(t)-1 and emit anchor-mismatch warning.
3) Cap ALL computations (VWAP/ATR/OBV/high/low/patterns) to bars with t ≤ t[k] (no look-ahead).

Anchor vs bar cross-check (non-blocking):
- If o[k] differs from LAST_O or c[k] from LAST_C by > $0.01: still use anchors; print:
  ⚠ Anchor mismatch: using provided last_open/last_close over bar[k].
- If last_volume missing, set LAST_V = v[k].

## Indicator Audit (objective table)
Create **Indicator Audit (1m/5m/15m)**:

Indicator | 1m last | 1m dir | 5m last | 5m dir | 15m last | 15m dir | Signal

- “last” uses primary field; most recent value up to k; rounded per rules.
- “dir” ∈ {rising, falling, flat} via slope over last 3 points (if <2 → "no data").

Signal rules (deterministic):
- RSI >55 bull; <45 bear; else neutral.
- MACD/MACDEXT: hist>0 bull; <0 bear; else neutral.
- STOCH/STOCHF/STOCHRSI: k>d and k rising → bull; k<d and k falling → bear; else neutral. (If d missing, k>55 bull, k<45 bear.)
- WILLR <−80 bear; >−20 bull; else neutral.
- ADX/ADXR: <20 neutral; ≥20 → +DI>−DI bull; −DI>+DI bear; (if DI missing → neutral).
- DI/DM: PLUSDI>MINUSDI & PLUSDM>MINUSDM → bull; inverse → bear; else neutral.
- MFI >55 bull; <45 bear; else neutral.
- CCI >+100 bull; <−100 bear; else neutral.
- MOM/ROC/ROCR/TRIX: primary>0 & rising → bull; <0 & falling → bear; else neutral.
- ULTOSC >50 bull; <45 bear; else neutral.
- AROON/AROONOSC: up>70 & down<30 → bull; reverse → bear; else neutral.
- MA family (SMA/EMA/WMA/DEMA): price (LAST_C for TF) > MA & MA rising → bull; price < MA & MA falling → bear; else neutral.
- BBANDS: LAST_C>middle & (upper−LAST_C)<(LAST_C−lower) → bull; LAST_C<middle & (LAST_C−lower)<(upper−LAST_C) → bear; else neutral.
- SAR: price above SAR → bull; below → bear.
- OBV/AD/ADOSC: rising 3 points while price up → bull; falling while price down → bear; else neutral.
- ATR/NATR/TRANGE/MIDPOINT/MIDPRICE/DX: neutral (range/regime only).

## NEW — Trade Decision Engine (BUY/SELL statements; levels required)
Using S, VWAP, S/R clusters, ATR5, and non-neutral signals:

### Trade types (rules)
- **Breakout Long** when:
  S>0, price above VWAP, ADX(5m)≥20, RSI(5m)>55,
  and 5m close > nearest resistance (R1).
  • Entry: buy stop at (R1 + 0.02).
  • Initial Stop: max{ R1 − 0.5×ATR5, nearest swing-low − 0.02 }.
  • Targets: T1 = R1 + 0.75×ATR5; T2 = Hmax or R2.
  • Trail: 0.6×ATR5 after +1R.

- **Pullback Long to VWAP/Support** when:
  S>0, price ≤ VWAP, oscillators show pullback (RSI(1m) 40–50 rising or STOCH cross up),
  and support S1 within 0.6×ATR5.
  • Entry: limit at S1 + 0.02 (or VWAP reclaim on 1m close).
  • Stop: S1 − 0.5×ATR5.
  • Targets: VWAP, then R1.
  • Trail: break-even after +1R, then 0.5×ATR5.

- **Breakdown Short** when:
  S<0, price below VWAP, ADX(5m)≥20, RSI(5m)<45,
  and 5m close < nearest support (S1).
  • Entry: sell stop at (S1 − 0.02).
  • Stop: min{ S1 + 0.5×ATR5, nearest swing-high + 0.02 }.
  • Targets: T1 = S1 − 0.75×ATR5; T2 = Lmin or S2.
  • Trail: 0.6×ATR5 after +1R.

- **Mean-Revert Short at Resistance** when:
  S ≥ 0 but overbought intraday (RSI(5m)>70 or STOCH>90) and price stalls at R1/R2 with OBV divergence.
  • Entry: limit at R1 − 0.02 after a 1m reversal signal.
  • Stop: R1 + 0.5×ATR5.
  • Targets: VWAP, then S1.
  • Trail: 0.5×ATR5.

## Forecast Adjustment Rulebook (ATR-bounded; unchanged)
Let ATR5 be 5m ATR(14) else scaled 1m ATR(14).
Compute S as above (w15m=0.45, w5m=0.35, w1m=0.20; clip ±1).
- ΔHigh = + min(2×ATR5, 0.6×ATR5 + 0.8×|S|×ATR5)
- ΔLow  = − min(2×ATR5, 0.6×ATR5 + 1.0×|S|×ATR5)
- ΔClose = sign(S) × min(ATR5, 0.4×ATR5 + 0.6×|S|×ATR5)

Adjustments:
- OBV divergence bearish: ΔLow −= 0.2×ATR5; ΔHigh −= 0.1×ATR5.
- Price < VWAP on both 5m & 15m: ΔHigh −= 0.2×ATR5.
- ADX<20 on both 5m & 15m: scale |ΔHigh| and |ΔLow| by 0.7.

Bounds & sanity:
- |High−Low| ≤ 2×ATR5 per row; Open_n = Close_{n−1} for n>1;
- Low ≤ min(Open,Close), High ≥ max(Open,Close); reduce deltas uniformly until valid; else table "no data".
